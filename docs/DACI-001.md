## Decision
**The team builds the Travel App**, Rishika's HW1 problem: a casual, quick way to review and rank the places you went and the things you did. Approved by Rishika on 2026-10-07.

Travel led on every criterion that depends on people rather than plumbing — reachability, meaning, and data we can actually get — and was second on buildability. The Music App scored highest on wickedness and lowest on buildability, which is the pattern of a problem worth having and hard to ship in three weeks. The Opt-in Directory was the most buildable and the least reachable: its users are employees at a specific company, and none of us can put four people in front of them by October 22.

**Runner-up: the Music App, 67.0.** Recorded so that the fallback is already decided if the reopen trigger fires.

## Dissent
No verbal dissent however: The spread between Travel (74.75) and the Opt-in Directory (64.0) is under 11 points on a 95-point scale, which is not a decisive margin. Individual scores on Meaning for the Directory ranged from 2 to 5, the widest disagreement anywhere in the table, so at least one member values that problem considerably more than the average suggests.

## What Would Reopen This
**By October 22, the team has not completed at least three interviews with real users of the Travel App.** Reachability was weighted at 4 and scored at 4.25 on the assumption that travelers are easy to find; if that proves false, the premise of the decision is gone and the fallback is the Music App, whose users are also the people in this room.
